Sales progress assessed separately from company quality

1. Executive summary

September produced 84 MQLs, down by 6 from 90 in August (-6.7%). Sales progress deserves separate attention: 16 leads, or 19.0%, were converted to Accounts, as confirmed by the business owner. This does not establish closed-won sales.

Organic search clicks recovered and average position improved. However, GA4 successful-form events fell from 119 to 99, so search growth did not translate into growth in total website inquiry events. Paid-search spend and clicks declined while CPC rose. Ads-recorded conversions increased from 4.61 to 9.00: higher CPC alone does not establish poorer campaign performance, and the nine conversions are not CRM MQLs.

LinkedIn impressions, Page Views and new followers all recovered. All six MQLs with GCLID values matched advertising clicks: five to the brand campaign and one to LoRaWAN. This confirms an advertising touch for those six records, not exclusive causation or attribution for the remaining 78.

7. Data quality, sources and limitations

  • MQL uses the primary Lead Type: 84 in September; 1 Technical and 2 Duplicate records are excluded. OTEIS and Crew Systems contain technical-question intent; retain source MQL labels and flag commercial intent for review rather than silently changing counts.
  • One additional MQL in the full source file has an unparseable creation date and cannot be assigned to a month; it is held in a private review list, outside September totals.
  • The supplemental export contains 117 records, including one additional GCLID-bearing record absent from the primary file; it does not increase the primary MQL total.
  • Full months use native platform dates: Ads and GA4 use Asia/Shanghai, LinkedIn uses UTC, and GSC uses its platform date convention. Cross-platform boundary days are not treated as identical.
  • Ads queries cover the complete month; 26 dates with metrics do not mean only 26 days of coverage. Omitted all-zero dates follow the completed full-month query; historical snapshots are not stacked.
  • August LinkedIn comparisons use the previously validated monthly summary; daily unique visitors are not summed into a monthly-unique metric.
  • Opportunity, order and revenue records are unavailable; no sales revenue, true ROI or acquisition cost based on the incomplete matched sample is calculated.
