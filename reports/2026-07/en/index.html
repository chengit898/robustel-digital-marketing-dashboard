1. Executive Summary

Reporting period: July 1, 2026 - July 31, 2026 | Prepared by: Sharleen | Status: Green

July was a healthy month. CRM MQLs increased from 62 in June to 90 in July, a +45.2% month-over-month increase. GA4 lead-success events also increased from 80 to 101.

Paid search behaved like a controlled-spend month. Google Ads spend decreased from $1,786 to $1,295, while CPC stayed broadly stable at $0.52. Because MQL volume increased, lower click volume should not be treated as a negative result by itself; the remaining blocker is attribution quality.

SEO was stable rather than expansionary: GSC clicks were 5,695, impressions were 299,632, and CTR moved from 1.92% to 1.90%. The next practical step is CTR and CTA optimization on high-intent pages.

LinkedIn recovered versus June, with impressions up +20.8% and company page views up +20.9%. Stronger content themes included LoRaWAN, project applications, building protocol integration, and industrial video scenarios.

Lead quality should be evaluated by account profile, market relevance, vertical fit, and buying maturity rather than stated quantity or product-model text. Representative high-quality accounts include JLL, Johnson Controls, DHL, SEMAR, NTT DATA, SNGPL, Camil Alimentos, Applied EV, LORIOT, OQ Technology, Progressive Impact Corporation, and SAFEgroup Automation.

8. Data Quality and Limitations

  • Missing GCLID / lead_tracking_id: this report supports trend and market diagnosis only. It does not attribute individual leads or calculate true Ads-to-MQL ROI.
  • CRM MQL and GA4 generate_lead_thankyou are reported separately. GA4 events are not deduplicated CRM leads.
  • Google Ads conversion fields are not treated as validated lead conclusions.
  • LinkedIn data comes from manual exports and remains upper-funnel context unless UTM and CRM attribution are fixed.
  • Lead tiering is management judgment based on supplied company, country, industry, and inquiry context. Sales must verify end customer, buyer role, project stage, and buying authority.
  • No raw email addresses are displayed in the published HTML.
