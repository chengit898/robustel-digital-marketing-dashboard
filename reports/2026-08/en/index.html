1. Executive Summary

August generated 90 CRM MQLs, unchanged from July. Volume was stable, while the account profile remained strategically useful across global enterprises, public infrastructure, research institutions, and specialist IoT or integration firms.

GA4 sessions increased from 30,141 to 32,261 (+7.0%), and generate_lead_thankyou events increased from 101 to 119 (+17.8%). GSC impressions rose +7.0%, but clicks fell -7.7%; CTR moved from 1.90% to 1.64%.

Google Ads spend was USD 1,283, clicks were 2,249, and CPC rose to USD 0.57. LinkedIn impressions fell to 7,233. LinkedIn page views reached 354 (-44.4% MoM), follower growth was 87 (-34.1%), and total unique visitors were 154.

Attribution boundary: GCLID / lead_tracking_id is missing. Trend and market diagnosis only; no individual-lead attribution or true Ads-to-MQL ROI.
