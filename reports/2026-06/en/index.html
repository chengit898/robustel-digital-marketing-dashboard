1. Executive Summary

Reporting period: June 1, 2026 - June 30, 2026 | Prepared by: Sharleen | Status: Yellow

June delivered modest MQL growth but required quality calibration. CRM MQLs increased from 58 in May to 62 in June, a +6.9% increase.

Paid search expanded materially: Google Ads spend rose from $1,404 to $1,786, and clicks rose from 2,825 to 3,503. MQL growth was much smaller than traffic growth, so the added click volume still requires search-term and campaign-level quality review.

GA4 showed stronger website engagement, with sessions increasing from 20,742 to 28,626 and generate_lead_thankyou events increasing from 70 to 80. Missing GCLID / lead_tracking_id prevents true lead-level attribution.

SEO improved in June: GSC clicks increased to 5,745, impressions increased to 298,565, and CTR improved from 1.82% to 1.92%.

Lead quality should be interpreted through account qualification rather than quantity statements in inquiry text. Representative accounts include Orica, YUNEX Traffic, Andersen Lab, Ceneco Green Power, Beit Al Etisalat, Services and Automation for Energy, and V4One. Review-category accounts should be validated before being upgraded.

8. Data Quality and Limitations

  • Missing GCLID / lead_tracking_id: this report supports trend and market diagnosis only. It does not attribute individual leads or calculate true Ads-to-MQL ROI.
  • CRM MQL and GA4 generate_lead_thankyou are reported separately. GA4 events are not deduplicated CRM leads.
  • Google Ads conversion fields are not treated as validated lead conclusions.
  • LinkedIn data comes from manual exports and remains upper-funnel context unless UTM and CRM attribution are fixed.
  • Lead tiering is management judgment based on supplied company, country, industry, and inquiry context. Sales must verify end customer, buyer role, project stage, and buying authority.
  • No raw email addresses are displayed in the published HTML.
